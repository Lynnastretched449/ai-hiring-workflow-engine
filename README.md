## What this tool does

The ai-hiring-workflow-engine helps you evaluate job candidates. It reads resume files and compares them against your job descriptions. You get a match score and a list of skill gaps. The tool also suggests ways to improve resumes to better fit specific roles. This process replaces manual screening and saves time for recruiters.

## ⚙️ How to use the software

The application uses a simple interface to process your documents. Follow this workflow to analyze your applicants.

1. Open the application. 
2. Upload your job description file in PDF or Word format.
3. Upload one or more resumes for comparison.
4. Click the Analyze button.
5. Wait for the engine to process the data.
6. Review the match scores for each candidate.
7. Click on individual candidates to see a list of missing skills.
8. Read the actionable feedback generated by the AI to understand how to fix the gaps.

## 📊 Understanding the results

The software provides three main metrics for every resume you upload.

### The match score
The match score represents how well a candidate aligns with your job description. A higher percentage indicates a strong fit. We calculate this by identifying shared keywords and professional experiences between both documents.

### Skill gaps
The software lists required skills from your job description that do not appear in the candidate resume. This section helps you identify if a candidate lacks specific technical tools or certifications necessary for the role.

### Actionable fixes
The engine identifies specific parts of the resume where the candidate should include more detail. Instead of telling you that a candidate is a bad fit, the software tells you what the candidate should add to be a better fit.

## 📁 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during the setup or usage, follow these steps to fix common problems.

* If the application does not open, restart your computer and try again.
* Ensure your files contain readable text rather than images. If a resume is a scanned image, the software cannot read it.
* Check your internet connection if the analysis fails to complete, as the engine requires a connection to verify its settings.
* If the application freezes, close it using the Task Manager and reopen it.

## 📝 Frequently asked questions

### Does this software save my resumes?
No. All files stay on your computer. The engine processes the text locally and does not store your files on external servers.

### Can I analyze fifty resumes at once?
Yes. You can upload batches of resumes. Keep in mind that a very large batch might take a few moments longer to finish the analysis.

### What if I need to change my job description?
You can update your job description at any time. Just upload the new document, and the software will re-rank your candidate list based on the new criteria.

### Do I need a subscription?
No. This tool is free to use. You do not need to sign up for accounts or pay for access to the features.

### Is my data secure?
The software handles your data locally. Since no data leaves your machine during the core analysis, your privacy remains intact.
